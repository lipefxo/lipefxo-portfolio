# Photographic texture generation

Built-in Image Gen generated these material albedos; originals retained as PNG and runtime copies converted to WebP at quality 88.

- [Masonry](../../public/terrain/textures/brick.webp), 1.2 m texture module.
- [Grass](../../public/terrain/textures/grass.webp), 4 m runtime repeat module.

## Masonry prompt

Seamless orthographic photographic albedo of warm sandy soil-cement masonry, running bond, 1.2m square, 30cm bricks, 16 courses, flat neutral light, pores and compressed earth grains, no objects or text.

## Grass prompt

Seamlessly tileable photorealistic PBR albedo texture of maintained Brazilian garden lawn, top down orthographic ground scan, 3x3 metre patch, fine green grass blades intermixed olive green and occasional straw fibers, lush but natural mid-dark green, subtly varied density. Uniform diffuse overcast neutral light, absolutely no cast shadows, no highlights, no perspective, no flowers, no objects, no borders or labels. Full image edge-to-edge lawn, match edges for seamless repeating material, avoid obvious clumps or repeating distinctive features. Crisp microdetail, professional scanned game architectural material texture, square1024x1024.

The albedo-derived bump maps are approximate surface detail, not scanned physical height data.
